Your Google Ads plan

Painting jobs up to 80 miles from Rome, smaller jobs within 40.

The plan

  • Stop paying for the wrong results. Google has been counting page views and direction requests as results, so its bidding chased those instead of calls and quote requests. We pause both Performance Max campaigns, stop Google from changing your campaigns on its own, cap what Search pays per click, and count only quote requests and calls of a minute or longer.
  • Three Search campaigns, each with its own budget. They replace your current Search and Smart campaigns. Atlanta and Chattanooga are inside 80 miles, and three counties there get more painting searches than the 18 counties around Rome combined. Separate budgets keep that outer area from using up the money meant for jobs close to home.
  • Only people ready to hire. Ads show for searches like "house painters" and "pressure washing near me," and each ad opens the matching page of your website. We block job seekers, do-it-yourselfers and people shopping for paint or equipment. Past 40 miles, ads skip searches for one room or a touch-up.
  • A quote form on every service page. Today the only form is on the Contact page, one extra click away from every ad.
  • Judged on real jobs. Every quote request and call from the ads is tracked by search and area. Tell us which ones became jobs, and we'll move money toward what brings them.
