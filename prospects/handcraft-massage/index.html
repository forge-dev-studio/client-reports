What's costing you

  • Google has 3 of your 13 main pages in its index. Your wedding packages, policies, gift cards, profile and five of your six service pages don't come up in Google at all.
  • Each service page carries 15 to 53 words of its own. The rest is the same footer, policy and FAQ text that appears on every page. There's no page for lymphatic drainage or deep tissue, though the site says you specialize in lymphatic drainage.
  • You showed up in the map pack on 15 of the 440 searches that displayed one, 9 of them in Adairsville. You weren't in the map pack for "massage", "massage near me", "best massage", "foot massage" or "deep tissue massage" in any town.
  • In Adairsville's own searches, Beyond Massage holds the top spot in 23 of the 32 that showed a map pack, with 2 reviews. Beyond Massage and your studio are both about half a mile from the center of town, so distance isn't what separates you.
  • You share Suite B with Narjé Massage and Wellness: same Google category, 86 reviews to your 7. Across these searches Narjé made the map pack 34 times and you made it 15, and Apple Maps shows Narjé first when someone searches your business name.
  • Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and Data Axle have no listing for you. Copilot and ChatGPT draw on Bing, Siri draws on Apple Maps, and Yelp's reviews feed ChatGPT and Perplexity.
  • Seven reviews so far, and the newest one we could date is from June 19.
  • You're closed Wednesdays, Saturdays and after 5:45 PM. All three businesses in Adairsville's "massage" map pack were open until 9 PM or later on the Thursday evening we searched, and Google favors businesses that are open when someone searches.
  • A few leftovers make the site look unattended: the Summer BOGO that ended July 31 still shows on all 17 pages and in the description your homepage gives Google, a Wix placeholder page ("Double click to edit and add your own text") is live at /services-4, and the TikTok icon opens Wix's own TikTok.
