Where the market stands

Inside Rome, three companies hold 102 of the 117 map pack spots and split the city by side of town. Outside Rome, no single company carries the ring. Each town has its own mix, and in 8 of the 28 towns most first places go to listings named after the town.

The searching isn't centered on Rome. Bartow County sends the most rental searches, about 370 a month counting Cartersville's, ahead of Floyd at about 260, Gordon at about 180 and Polk at about 80.

What stands out

  • Websites and map packs don't line up. mulkeydumpsterrental.com shows on page one for 115 of the 252 searches, more than any other dumpster company's site, and it's the top web result for "dumpster rental cartersville ga" and "dumpster rental calhoun ga." Mulkey's map pack spots come from 6 towns, none of them Cartersville or Calhoun.
  • Elite Dumpsters has no website on its Google listing and is still the most-shown business in the ring: 75 map pack spots across 9 towns.
  • Cartersville's search, "dumpster rental cartersville ga," is the biggest town search after Rome at about 90 a month, and all three of its map pack spots went to town-name listings. Calhoun's, at about 50 a month, showed 2 town-name listings and Smith Holdings Roll Off Dumpsters.
  • Rockmart is held by local companies. All 33 of its map pack spots went to All Styles Dumpster Rental, VPS Dumpster Rental and Black Rock Roll Off.
  • Google's AI answer appeared on 64 of the 252 searches. It named Waste Management most often (26), then Mulkey (14), Elite Dumpsters (13) and VPS Dumpster Rental (12).
  • Mulkey was the only company with a sponsored listing inside the map pack. On "dumpster rental rome ga," Local Services ads from two hauling companies sat above everything else on the page.

How this was measured. 252 Google searches on the evening of September 26, 2026, between 8:04 and 8:16 PM Eastern, each reading the map pack, the top 20 web results and Google's AI answer.
