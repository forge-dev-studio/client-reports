The market in brief

  • Inside Rome, Elite Dumpsters, Mulkey and Dumpsters of Rome hold 102 of the 117 map pack spots, and each leads the side of town nearest its address.

How this was measured. 252 Google searches on the evening of September 26, 2026, between 8:04 and 8:16 PM Eastern, each reading the map pack, the top 20 web results and Google's AI answer. Plain searches like "dumpster rental near me" ran from 21 places: five spots around Rome and every town in the ring Google treats as a city. Searches that name a town ran from that town, for every Census town and community within 30 miles of Rome. For every search that showed a map pack, Google's full list of local businesses was read right after, which is where "4th to 6th" comes from. Sponsored listings are left out of every count. We checked the method against a real browser search in Rome first: 4 of 5 town-name searches matched exactly and the fifth showed the same three businesses with the first two swapped. Monthly search counts come from Google's advertising data. Ratings and reviews are as Google showed them that evening. Town-name listings are listings named after a town or with a first name and initial, with three reviews or fewer and, for most, no website.
