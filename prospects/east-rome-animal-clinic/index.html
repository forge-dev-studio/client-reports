What's working

  • 4.8 stars from 231 Google reviews, with new ones still coming in (the newest we could date is from August).
  • In Rome, the map pack shows you for "vet near me" (third) and "veterinarian near me" (second), and first for "low cost vet rome ga". Searching from the south side of Rome put you first for both "vet near me" and "veterinarian near me".
  • In Lindale you're the first clinic in the map pack for "vet near me", "veterinarian near me", "veterinarian", "vet lindale ga" and "vet clinic lindale ga", and in the map pack for 10 Lindale searches in all.
  • Your website is on page one of Google's regular results for 8 of the 12 plain searches from Rome and 5 of the 6 that name Rome, and second for "low cost vet rome ga".
  • Google's AI answer names you on 9 searches, including "low cost vet rome ga" and "cheap vet near me" from Cedartown and Rockmart.
  • Your name and phone number match on your site, Google, Bing, Yelp and Facebook, and your hours match on your site and Google. The site is secure and every page has tap-to-call.
  • Your Facebook page is active: 1.3K followers and 96% recommend from 23 reviews.
  • 73 other websites link to yours, in the same range as Lakeview (75) and Possum Trot (80).

What's costing you

  • The map pack included you on 13 of the 315 searches that showed one, all of them from Rome or Lindale. In Rome itself you made it on 3 of 15.
  • Typing the town in drops you out. "vet rome ga", which Georgia searches about 720 times a month counting its variations, shows Culbreth Carr Watson Animal Clinic, Etowah Animal Hospital and West Rome Animal Clinic. "animal hospital rome ga" shows the emergency center, Etowah and Possum Trot. You weren't in either, and "emergency vet rome ga" showed no map pack at all.
  • Where someone stands in Rome matters. From five spots around town, you showed up for "vet near me" in 2, "animal hospital" in 1 and "veterinarian" in none.
  • Your Google category is "Animal hospital". Of the other seven Rome clinics we checked, six are listed as "Veterinarian", which matches "vet" and "veterinarian", the words in most of these searches.
  • Northwest Georgia Veterinary Emergency Center made the map pack on 94 of these searches in 24 towns, more than any clinic, including 41 everyday searches like "animal hospital near me".
  • Outside Rome and Lindale, the map pack didn't include you in any of the other 24 towns, and your website reached Google's top 20 from just one of them (Shannon, for "low cost vet shannon ga"). Other Floyd County clinics do reach past their own town: Cave Spring Animal Hospital made Cedartown's map pack 9 times.
  • Google has 12 of your 15 pages. Wellness Exams, Flea and Tick, and Puppy and Kitten aren't in its index, and an old /about address still is.
  • Page titles are bare names like "Surgery" and "Wellness Exams", and none of your service pages mention Rome or "vet". Four pages have no search description.
  • There's no page of its own for vaccinations, spay and neuter, dental care or emergencies. Spay, neuter and dental sit inside the Surgery page. The Gallery page shows "Photo Gallery" and "Instagram" headings with nothing under them.
  • On a mid-range phone your homepage takes about 4 seconds to show its main content and weighs 1.7 MB.
  • Yelp lists you as unclaimed, with 2.0 stars from 4 reviews against your 4.8 on Google, and we didn't see replies from the clinic on your recent Google reviews.
  • You're closed Saturdays. Eight of the ten other general practices in Floyd County open Saturday mornings.
  • Google Analytics only starts after a visitor accepts the cookie banner, so visits from people who ignore it aren't counted.
