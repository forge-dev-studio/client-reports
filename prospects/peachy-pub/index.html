Where you stand

Google already knows you as Rome's pub. It doesn't yet know you as a place to eat, and food is where the searching is: about 54 food searches for every bar search in Floyd County. A closed business's listing at your address is still competing with you too.

Your three goals, measured

You told us you want promotion and more traffic, in the door and online.

Traffic in the door

About 1,120 of the 64,010 monthly searches we measured for places to eat and drink around Rome show you in the map pack, the three places Google lists first. Nearly all of those are bar searches. Food searches show you about 70 times a month.

Promotion

Your events don't reach Google. Its events list for "live music rome ga" showed 10 events and none of yours. You're not on Visit Rome, the Downtown Rome dining directory, TripAdvisor's Rome nightlife list or Yelp's top 10 bars in Rome.

Social

8,700 Facebook followers, the most of any downtown bar we could read, and 2,511 on Instagram, about twice the downtown middle. The audience is there. Someone who doesn't follow you yet won't find your events on Google, and your Yelp page shows no reviews.

Why food is the gap

On the food searches we ran from your own front door, where being closest counts for you, Google didn't list you in its top 20 on 16 of 21.

Review count doesn't explain it. On food searches from Rome, 187 of the 453 map pack spots went to places with fewer Google reviews than your 110.

Where you're close: third for "burgers rome ga", fourth for "pizza rome ga", eighth for "food rome ga" and "wings rome ga", ninth for "lunch near me" downtown, and eleventh for "restaurants rome ga", the biggest food search that names Rome at 12,100 a month.

What's working

  • Google knows you as Rome's pub. "pub" put you first from all 9 spots we searched from. "bar near me" put you first downtown, at Google's Rome point and in south Rome, and second or third across the rest of town. "sports bar near me" and "karaoke near me" showed you from 8 of the 9 spots.
  • Searches that name the town work too. "bars rome ga" and "karaoke rome ga" show you first, and "bars rome ga" still shows you third when it's searched from Cartersville, Calhoun and Cedartown. "bars shannon ga" and "bars lindale ga" put you first.
  • Late-night searches do show you as a place to eat. "eateries open late near me" put you in the map pack from all 6 Rome spots, first downtown and in south Rome, and "food open late near me" showed you in every map pack it had, first downtown. "bars open late near me" put you first at Google's Rome point and downtown.
  • 4.3 stars from 110 Google reviews, and the reviews Google features praise the staff, the quick service and the food.
  • Your full menu is on your site as real text with prices, and Google's panel shows menu highlights with Order pickup and Order delivery buttons.
  • 59 other websites link to yours, level with NYC Eats (64) and Giggity's (63).

What's costing you

A closed business still lives at your address

Google has two profiles at 325 Broad St: yours, and "Peaches", a live music venue that has closed. Its profile shows 613 reviews, says it's open, and lists a different phone number and a website that no longer works. From Atlanta, Chattanooga, Cartersville, Calhoun and Cedartown, "live music rome ga" puts Peaches in the map pack, not you. Around Rome, "live music near me" shows Peaches in the map pack from 5 of 6 spots, while you were 8th to 10th in Google's full list. Peaches also still has a Facebook page with 7,500 followers that reads "Open every Thurs, Fri & Sat", a Bing listing and a TripAdvisor page.

  • Your hours disagree with each other. Google says you close at 2 AM, Yelp and MapQuest say 11 PM on weekdays, DoorDash says 1:10 AM, and your own homepage lists both 11 PM and 2 AM, with a weekend line that reads "10:00PM - 12:00AM".
  • A Google search of your site turns up 3 of its 43 pages: the homepage and two pizza pages. Your menu pages aren't among them, there's no events page, and the site's code doesn't tell Google your hours or menu.
  • Yelp lists you as unclaimed with no reviews showing, and TripAdvisor has no page for you. On Google, we didn't see owner replies on the reviews we could read, or any owner updates or events.
  • In our phone test, your homepage took 4.9 seconds to show its main content and the menu page 8.4. A "Hello world!" placeholder post with a spam comment is live on the site.
  • Google's AI answer appeared on 43 of the 356 searches and named you on 4.
