Where you stand

Google already treats you as Rome's pub: first for "pub" everywhere we searched from, first for "bar near me" downtown. But food is where the searching is, about 54 searches for food for every one for a bar, and there the map pack almost never shows you. A closed business's listing at your address still competes with you for live music.

Your three goals, measured

You told us you want promotion and more traffic, in the door and online. Here is what we found for each.

Traffic in the door

The map pack, the three places Google lists at the top of a search, shows you on about 1,120 of the 64,010 searches a month we measured for places to eat and drink in and around Rome. Nearly all of those are bar searches. Food searches are 98% of the searching, and they show you on about 70.

Promotion

Your events don't reach Google. Google's events list for "live music rome ga" showed ten events and none of yours, and for "live music rome ga this weekend" the map pack shows Moon Roof Bar, Dark Side of the Moon and Peaches, a closed business at your address. You're not on Visit Rome, the Downtown Rome dining directory, TripAdvisor's Rome nightlife list or Yelp's top 10 bars in Rome.

Social

Your Facebook audience is the largest of any downtown bar we could read: 8,700 followers, ahead of Cosmic Dog Outpost's 8,400. Instagram has 2,511 followers, about twice the downtown middle. The audience is there. The places people look when they don't know you yet, Google's events, Yelp and TripAdvisor, don't show you.

Is Google sure who you are?

Every map pack, AI answer and "open now" filter starts from the listings Google trusts for your address.

  • Google has two profiles at 325 Broad St. Yours, "Peachy Pub & Grill" (Bar & grill, 4.3 stars, 110 reviews), and "Peaches" (Live music venue, 4.0 stars, 613 reviews), a closed business whose profile still says it's open, with a different phone number and a website that no longer works.
  • Search "peaches rome ga" and Google's panel shows Peaches as open. From Atlanta, Chattanooga, Cartersville, Calhoun and Cedartown, "live music rome ga" puts Peaches in the map pack, second or third, and not you. So does "live music rome ga this weekend" read from a browser.
  • The leftover listing reaches past Google: Peaches still has a Facebook page with 7,500 followers that reads "Open every Thurs, Fri & Sat", an Instagram account, a Bing listing, a Yelp page marked closed and an empty TripAdvisor page.
  • Your own details don't agree with each other. You're "Peachy Pub & Grill" on Google, your site, Facebook and Instagram, and "Peachy Pub" on Yelp, DoorDash and Uber Eats. Your closing time reads 2 AM on Google, 11 PM on Yelp and MapQuest, 1:10 AM on DoorDash, and both 11 PM and 2 AM on your own homepage, where the weekend line says "10:00PM - 12:00AM".
  • Searches for your own name work: "peachy pub", "peachy pub rome", "peachy pub rome ga" and "peachy pub menu" all bring up your profile.

What's working

  • Google treats you as Rome's pub. "pub" put you first in the map pack from all 9 spots we searched from, and "bar near me" put you first from downtown, Google's own Rome point and south Rome, and second or third from the rest of town and Shannon.
  • "sports bar near me" and "karaoke near me" put you in the map pack from 8 of the 9 spots. "bars rome ga" and "karaoke rome ga" show you first and "rome bar" second, and "bars rome ga" shows you third even when it's searched from Cartersville, Calhoun and Cedartown.
  • "bars shannon ga" and "bars lindale ga" put you first, searched from inside each town.
  • Counting every search run from Rome, you're sixth of all the businesses Google showed, with 31 map pack spots and 12 first places, ahead of every other bar.
  • 4.3 stars from 110 Google reviews, and the reviews Google features praise the staff, quick service and the food. Google tags 8 of them with karaoke.
  • Your full menu is on your site as real text with prices, across 13 menu pages, and Google's panel shows menu highlights with Order pickup and Order delivery buttons.
  • Your Facebook page has 8,700 followers, more than any downtown bar we could read, and a dated upcoming event. Instagram has 2,511.
  • Your website is on page one for 11 searches, all bar searches, including second for "pub" from Rome.
  • 59 other websites link to yours, in the same range as NYC Eats (64) and Giggity's (63). The site is secure, tap-to-call is on every page and it's connected to Google Search Console.

What's costing you

  • Food is where the searching is, and the map pack doesn't show you there. "restaurants near me", "food near me", "pizza near me", "wings near me", "lunch near me" and "buffet near me" left you out from all 9 spots, and so did the other 16 plain food searches we ran. Your one food spot was third for "burgers rome ga". The nearest misses were fourth in Google's full list, for "pizza rome ga" from Rome and for "restaurants shannon ga" and "food shannon ga" from Shannon.
  • Google has 3 pages of your site in its index: the homepage and two pizza pages. The 13 menu pages with your prices aren't in it.
  • A closed business's profile at your address, with 613 reviews and "open" hours, takes the "live music rome ga" spots you'd expect to hold.
  • Yelp lists you as unclaimed with no recommended reviews, and there's no TripAdvisor page for you at all.
  • We didn't see an owner reply on the Google reviews we could read, and your Google profile shows no owner updates or events. The newest update is a visitor's, from about 7 months ago.
  • On a mid-range phone your homepage takes 4.9 seconds to show its main content and weighs 4.7 MB. The menu page takes 8.4 seconds, and the reservation page jumps around as it loads.
  • The site's "Hello world!" placeholder post is live and open to Google, with a spam comment linking to an unrelated site. The catering menu page, linked from every page, has no menu on it.
  • There's no events page on your site, and the site's code doesn't tell Google your hours, menu or that you're a restaurant and bar.
