What this is, and what it is not

Journey Electric does not yet have a Google Business Profile or a website, so there is nothing of yours to measure. This is not a ranking report. It is a picture of the room: what people in this market actually search for when they need an electrician, and who currently answers.

Method. Twenty four electrician search terms were priced against Google Ads demand data for Georgia and for Rome. The ten with the most demand were then searched live in Rome, and the top eight local results plus the top eight web results were recorded for each. That is 20 live searches, captured July 28, 2026.

What the landscape says

In your favour

  • These clicks are worth far more than handyman clicks. The ten electrician terms average $15.84 per click. Most handyman terms in the same market sit well below that. Electrical work is bought by people who have already decided to pay somebody.
  • A separate profile is the right instinct. Google ranks a business on the category it is registered under. A handyman profile and an electrical profile compete for different searches, and one profile trying to do both will underperform at both.
  • The web results are thin. Across the ten terms, directories take a large share of the first page, which means the number of genuine electrical websites competing is smaller than the review counts alone suggest.
  • Position is not bought with reviews alone. Source Electrical Contractors averages position 2.5 on 60 reviews, while Matthews Lighting and Electric, LLC carries 246 reviews and averages 3.4. Being newer is not the barrier it looks like.
  • The existing handyman profile already picks up electrical work. In Rome it appears for ceiling fan installation at position 9, recessed lighting installation at position 5, outlet installation at position 6. There is demonstrated relevance to build on rather than a cold start.

What you would be walking into

  • This field is more review heavy than the handyman field. Median review count among the 8 electrical contractors holding these results is 60, against 14 among the businesses holding the Rome handyman results. The deepest electrical profile carries 246, against 87 on the handyman side.
  • 2 businesses appear in all 10 searches. Source Electrical Contractors, Matthews Lighting and Electric, LLC hold position across the whole category rather than in one corner of it.
  • A new profile starts with no review history. The businesses above have been accumulating theirs for years. That gap is the biggest structural difference between launching this listing and the handyman one, which already carries 38 reviews.
  • There is already a "Tanner Electric" in this market. It appears in the Rome results at an average position of 5.11 across 9 of the 10 searches, and it took first place from the south Rome measurement point. Given that shares your own first name, branding this division on "Tanner" would put you in direct name competition with an established listing. "Journey Electric" avoids that, and it carries the parent brand across, so reviews and recognition earned under Journey Home Services point the same direction rather than at a stranger.
  • Category and licence details decide what the profile can rank for. Google asks electrical contractors for licence information in some cases, and the category chosen at signup determines which of the ten searches above the listing is eligible for at all. Worth settling before the profile is created rather than after.
  • The name itself still needs a formal check. Nothing here verifies that "Journey Electric" is clear on the Georgia Secretary of State register or as a mark. That is a registry and legal question, not something search data can answer, and it is worth resolving before signage and vehicle lettering are committed.
This is a landscape, not a plan. It describes the market as it stood on July 28, 2026. Happy to walk through any single search behind it.
