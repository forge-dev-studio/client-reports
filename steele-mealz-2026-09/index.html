Can a brand-new catering business in Rome, Georgia be built on search? Measured demand across a 60-mile service radius, plus the Rome competitive field up close.

Client Chef Russell Steele Entity Steel Food Service LLC, dba Steele Mealz Catering Measured 2026-09-02 Coverage 60 towns · 3 states

Verdict — Proceed

The demand is real, the competition is thin, and the field is open. Build it as an organic play, not a map-pack play.

Georgia generates 25,260 catering searches a month across 319 distinct queries, and 1,470 of those are reachable from Rome. Nearly half the query set carries a difficulty score of zero, the head term sits at 8, and advertisers pay a median of $3.17 a click for this traffic — three independent signals that the demand converts and nobody has claimed it.

The condition on that verdict: the bulk of the money sits 30 to 60 miles out, in the band where the map pack stops reaching and only organic search does. The Rome pack is real and worth winning — all eight core queries return one, and position one is held on 51 to 67 reviews — but it is the near market, not the market.

Strongest markets in the radius

    Rome ranks 20th of 60 by measured demand. Of the 19 towns ahead of it, all but Cartersville (22mi) and Dallas (30mi) sit more than 30 miles out.

    Do not add the town figures together. Google's city targets are radius-based and overlap heavily — the 46 Georgia towns sum to 63,320/mo against a state total of 25,260, which is 2.5x double-counting. The town numbers are valid for comparing towns to each other. The non-overlapping market size is the state figure.

    For the same reason, read Rome's 1,470/mo as demand reachable from Rome rather than demand inside the city limits. Google's Rome target returns 5.8% of Georgia's catering demand while Rome-Floyd holds roughly 0.9% of the state's population, so the target clearly extends well past the county line.

    The distance problem, stated plainly

    Map-pack presence decays with distance in a way that is measured, not assumed. On a prior Forge build the rate of holding pack position one collapsed from 62% to 5% between the 0–10 mile band and the 30–45 mile band. Organic position one fell far more gently, and ranking organically at all held at 43% out at 30–45 miles.

    Set that against where this market's demand lives and the strategy writes itself.

    It also sets a boundary worth stating at the sale rather than at the ninety-day review: Atlanta and Chattanooga are inside the drive radius, not the year-one ranking target. A brand-new site with no authority does not rank for catering in either metro. They belong in the service-area schema and the copy, not in the forecast.

    Corporate catering underdelivered

    It was the largest block in the seed taxonomy — 80 of 378 seeds — and the most intuitive thing to sell: recurring revenue, a ten-person minimum that matches an office lunch order exactly, and a Rome employer base of three colleges and two hospital systems. It measured 510 searches a month, 2% of demand. The queries are real and uncontested, and they may convert well, but they are not an engine and should not be pitched as one.

    Cuisine beat event type

    Cuisine-anchored queries carry roughly twice the demand of event-type queries — breakfast catering at 480, bbq catering near me at 480, mexican food catering near me at 390. For a chef whose range runs from fine dining to soul food, that is a fortunate result: the pages that carry the most demand are the ones he is best placed to write.

    Difficulty is near zero. Of 319 queries, 148 score 0 and 214 score 15 or below. The largest local-intent term in the market, catering near me at 3,600 a month, scores 8.

    The review moat is shallow. Filtering Google's Georgia business listings to companies whose primary category is actually catering, the largest has 1,267 reviews and most sit under 200. The restaurants that crowd an unfiltered list carry five to nine thousand. Starting from zero reviews is a far smaller handicap in catering than in most local trades.

    No dominant regional operator exists. The largest single category in Georgia's catering listings is "mobile caterer" — 88 of 414 — and it is populated by coffee, dessert and taco trucks. Small operators, no consolidator.

    Three findings that change how this gets built.

    1. The map pack is real and it is winnable. All eight core catering queries return a local pack in Rome, so GBP work is not optional — it is the near-market engine. And the bar is low: pack position one is held by businesses with 51 to 67 reviews, not hundreds.

    2. The number one pack holder has no website at all. Helecia's takes position one on six of eight queries with zero web presence. Pack rank in Rome is being decided on category, proximity and reviews — which means a competitor can be outranked in the pack before a single page is written, and separately outranked in organic where they cannot compete at all.

    3. Organic in Rome is unclaimed by caterers. Three of the four local caterers with websites rank for fewer than 45 keywords each. The organic results are held instead by aggregators — Facebook on 13 of the placements, Yelp on 9, The Knot on 9, ezCater on 7 — and by businesses that are not caterers at all, including the Rome city government site and an Applebee's location page.

    The case, both ways

    What argues for building

    • 25,260 searches a month in Georgia across 319 distinct queries, with 1,470 reachable from Rome.
    • 148 queries at zero difficulty, and the head term at 8. Nobody has defended this vertical.
    • Advertisers pay $3.17 median per click, up to $18.69 on corporate lunch terms. Independent evidence the traffic converts.
    • Shallow review moat. Georgia's biggest genuine caterer holds 1,267 reviews; most hold under 200.
    • Greenfield. Zero existing web presence means no legacy site, no bad citations, and no migration risk.
    • The Rome field is thin. 19 caterers within 30 miles, median 16 reviews, 8 with no website at all — including the business currently holding pack position one.
    • An uncopyable content angle. He runs both a kitchen and a truck, so he can answer food truck vs catering credibly, which neither a pure caterer nor a pure truck operator can.

    What has to be accepted

    • This is an organic build. 62,440/mo sits outside the pack's reach against 11,790/mo inside it. Budget and timeline follow content, not GBP alone.
    • Corporate catering is not the engine at 2% of demand, despite being the most natural pitch.
    • Atlanta and Chattanooga are drive radius, not ranking targets in year one.
    • Wedding catering is aggregator territory. The organic results belong to The Knot, WeddingWire and Zola; a new domain will not displace them quickly.
    • Venue keywords are off the table. His Callahan Street space is also an event space, but River Arts Venue is a live Forge retainer a mile away. 398 venue queries were excluded on that basis.
    • No AI Overview surface. Zero of eight Rome catering queries return one, so there is no overview citation play here, unlike other trades.

    What is now answered, and what still is not

    An earlier draft of this report listed three open questions. The Rome audit answered two of them, and one answer reverses a recommendation.

    Answered — a local pack exists. All eight core catering queries return one in Rome. GBP work is the near-market engine, not a formality.

    Answered — who holds it. Helecia's, Season Events and El Arco, on 51 to 67 reviews between them.

    Answered, and it reverses a recommendation. The earlier draft called AI Overview capture the highest-value follow-up, reasoning from a prior build where overviews fired on 48% of measured cells. Zero of the eight Rome catering queries return an AI Overview. Catering is a local transactional intent that Google is still answering with a map pack and links, so there is no overview to be cited in and no work to do there. That recommendation is withdrawn.

    Still open. Pack existence was measured in Rome only. Whether the same holds across bands C and D — where the demand concentrates and where the pack decays anyway — is unmeasured, though it matters less given the pack does not reach those towns regardless. Measuring the outer ring would cost roughly $1.50 at $0.004 per cell.

    Method. 378 hand-built seed keywords across nine families expanded to 26,033 candidates via DataForSEO's keyword database, mined against 40 Georgia catering domains, filtered to 13,635 catering-relevant terms, then reduced to 319 distinct queries. Search volume measured at United States, Georgia state and 60 individual town scopes. A Rome audit added the full Google Business listings roster within 30 miles, live SERPs for eight core catering queries at Rome, and authority profiles of the ranking competitors. 337 API calls, $18.34.

    On the filtering. A raw pull of this market reads 814,000/mo, which is wrong by 32x. Chain brands contributed 305,090/mo — cookout menu is a restaurant chain, and chick-fil-a catering appeared in nine spellings at 12,100 each. Diner intent contributed 42,650/mo. The largest distortion was close variants: catering near me returned six spellings each reporting 3,600/mo, and Google reports one volume per variant cluster. The final figures count each cluster once, and admit a query only when every token in it is known catering vocabulary.
