What you lose falls into two tight groups. Two things stand out, and the second is the more important.
Chattanooga alone accounts for 750 searches a month, more than Rome and Marietta combined, which makes it the single largest market in the territory.

And across all 31 markets, the business terms column is almost entirely empty. Searches that pair a town with "brand photographer", "business headshots" or "corporate headshots" return essentially zero everywhere. Business clients do not search that way. They search "professional headshots" or "headshots near me" and let Google work out where they are, which is why the business lane is won through your Google profile and your service pages rather than through town-by-town pages.
