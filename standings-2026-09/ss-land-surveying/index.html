What this measures

Every number in this report is the position of your business in Google’s local results for one specific search in one specific town, for example "land surveyor Cedartown GA". Positions 1 to 3 mean the business shows in the map pack, the three-business box at the top of the results. A higher number means it ranks lower on the local results page, and a dot means it did not show up there at all. We run the same 20 searches in each of the 30 towns within 35 miles of Cedartown, every month, from a searcher’s point of view in that town. Your business was already established when we began tracking, so we show where it stands now rather than a made-up starting point.
