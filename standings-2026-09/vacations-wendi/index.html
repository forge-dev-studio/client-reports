What this measures

Every number in this report is the position of your business in Google’s local results for one specific search in one specific town, for example "disney travel agent Cave Spring GA". Positions 1 to 3 mean the business shows in the map pack, the three-business box at the top of the results. A higher number means it ranks lower on the local results page, and a dot means it did not show up there at all. We run the same 20 searches in each of the 25 towns within 35 miles of Cave Spring, every month, from a searcher’s point of view in that town. Because the website did not exist before July 4, 2026, every placement below was earned from an unranked start.
