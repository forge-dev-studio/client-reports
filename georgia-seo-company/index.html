Where we stand

The profile is winning and the website is not. A three-day-old verified profile already holds 17 packs and 10 first places, while 262 indexed pages pull 7 clicks a quarter at an average position of 59.4.

The scorecard reads 70 of 100, up from 62 on the 2026-09-21 audit. NAP on site, Google Business Profile and Backlinks were re-scored on this week's evidence: the address and phone measured across all 264 built pages, the verified profile now holding packs, and the new link count. The other five are unchanged. That audit's build categories still average 84.8; its market-facing categories average 46.1.

What's working

  • 17 of 114 searches show us in the map pack, 10 of those at #1, up from zero five weeks ago.
  • Rome alone: 6 of 6 searches show a pack, first place on 3, including "seo company" at #1 ahead of Strategic Visibility and TENDO Technologies LLC.
  • "SEO company" is the strongest term: 6 of 18 pack appearances, 4 first places.
  • Organic visibility is climbing too: 11 of 114 searches now show the site in Google's top 20, up from 3.
  • 162 backlinks from 19 domains today, up from 9 links and 5 domains at the prior baseline. The footer credits are landing.
  • All three re-scored categories moved up: NAP on site 77 (was 48), Google Business Profile 70 (was 55), Backlinks 28 (was 12).

What's not

  • 17 pack appearances land in 7 of the 19 towns: Rome, Cedartown, Summerville, Cartersville, Calhoun, Ellijay and Ringgold. The other 12, Dalton to Atlanta, show zero.
  • Three service lines barely register: "web design company", "web designer", "digital marketing agency" each land just 1 pack, none of them first place.
  • North Georgia Web Design holds a pack we miss in 7 cells on 2 reviews; Fame Internet Marketing in 6 cells on 40.
  • Three months of Search Console: 7 clicks, 7,600 impressions, a 0.1% click-through rate, average position 59.4 across 1,000 queries.
  • Our own name undersells us: "georgia seo company" averages position 23.8 on 67 impressions and 0 clicks.
  • 44 keywords rank site-wide, none on page one, about 6 visits a month across 262 pages.

AI search presence

The same 114 searches were checked for an AI answer above the results, in both capture windows.

  • An AI answer appeared on 14 of 114 searches today, down from 22 five weeks ago.
  • None of those 14 cited georgiaseocompany.com. AI answers citing us held at 0 in both windows.
  • "Local SEO" draws the most AI answers, 6 of 14. "SEO company," our strongest pack keyword, draws none.

AI answers are shrinking here, not growing, and none of them mention us yet. The searches that already show us in the map pack are the ones worth watching first.

What we do next

Four moves this snapshot points to, at a high level, not a how-to.

1

Push past the home cluster

All 17 pack appearances sit in Rome, Cedartown, Summerville, Cartersville, Calhoun, Ellijay and Ringgold. We test the nearest zero towns next, Dalton and Canton.

2

Fix NAP on the site itself

NAP on site is our weakest build category, 77 of 100, unrelated to either re-score this week. That one is ours to fix directly.

3

Turn indexed pages into clicks

258 of 261 pages are indexed and on-page scores 88 of 100, yet three months produced 7 clicks at position 59.4. Depth without rankings.

4

Re-run this measurement on a schedule

This snapshot sits five weeks after the last one, and the movement between them proved the profile work. We repeat the same 114 searches on the same schedule.

How this was measured. 114 Google searches (6 keywords by 19 towns) run through DataForSEO on September 23, 2026, reading Google's local pack and organic results, coordinate targeting, organic/live/advanced local_pack, Method C r=10km, depth 20 (same as 2026-08-19). The same 114 searches were run the same way on August 19, 2026 for the five-week comparison above. Search Console and the keyword footprint were read directly on September 23, 2026, at no cost. Rankings move day to day: the pattern across 114 searches is the signal, not any single cell.
