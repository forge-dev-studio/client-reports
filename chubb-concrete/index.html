How we would help

Four areas this measurement points to, at a high level, not a how-to.

1

Confirm which markets are worth winning

Demand ranges from 20 to 1,900 searches a month across the 10 services measured, and pack activity is not spread evenly across the 11 towns. A short list of what actually matters narrows where attention belongs.

2

Fix what a new visitor meets first

Before anyone reads about concrete work, they can meet a browser security warning, or a listing whose website opens a parked domain page. What happens in those first seconds is worth its own review.

3

Bring the two profiles and the town pages into line

One phone number sits under two inconsistent Google listings, and the eight town pages read 62 to 63 percent alike. Both are worth sorting out.

4

Put basic measurement in place, then re-check this on a schedule

Nothing on the site tracks a call or a lead today. This report is one snapshot, taken September 23, 2026: repeating the same search set on a schedule is what shows movement.
