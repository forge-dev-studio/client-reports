What this measures

A Google "local pack" is the short list of three businesses Google shows above the regular results when someone searches for a service near them. Being fourth means being invisible.

This report measured 110 searches: every one of your 10 services paired with every one of 11 towns, each search run against Google's local pack for that town's exact location, not a generic statewide result. Four of the same searches were then checked by hand in a browser the same day, and reproduced exactly: your Cedartown "concrete contractor" and "concrete driveway" searches both showed you at #1.

Where this points next

Four areas this measurement points to, in order of altitude, not detail.

1

Decide which towns are worth pursuing

11 towns were measured here, and they do not carry equal opportunity. A short list of which of them actually matter to the business narrows where any future attention belongs.

Before a visitor reaches anything about concrete work, they can meet a security warning or a parked domain instead. What happens in those first seconds is worth its own review.

Eight nearly identical town pages and two separate, inconsistent Google profiles are both findings on their own; how much each one matters is worth working out.

4

This report is a single snapshot taken on September 23, 2026. Repeating the same 110 searches on a regular schedule is what shows which towns and services move, and by how much.
