Google Ads strategy

How the ads and your free Google presence split the work.

The goal: the most qualified leads for the money. Real jobs, in your area, for decks, fences and retaining walls, porches and pergolas.

The rule that follows from it: pay for the customers Google isn't already sending you for free.

How the ads work

  • Search ads only. They reach people typing what they want into Google. No display or video ads.
  • We choose the searches and the price. Searches like "deck builder near me," "fence company near me" and "porch builder near me" get the most room. Price shoppers, do-it-yourselfers and job seekers are blocked.
  • Every town starts even. No town gets extra money until its leads earn it.
  • Leads are judged on quality. An estimate request or a call of a minute or longer counts as a lead. You tell us which ones were real jobs, and the money follows those.
  • The website closes. Your reviews sit at the top of every service page, and the estimate button follows visitors down the page.

Budget

$25 a day to start: $10 for decks, $10 for fences and retaining walls, and $5 for a new campaign for porches and pergolas. Decks and fences: about 50 clicks and 2 to 5 leads a month, about $600 a month paid to Google. Porches and pergolas: about 5 to 10 clicks a month, about $45 to $90 a month paid to Google, with a lead about every one to three months. We raise it only when two things are true: the leads are turning into jobs at a cost that works for you, and the budget is running out before the searches do.

What happens when

Before launch

A test call and a test form with you, so every lead reaches you.

First 30 days

Block bad searches, set the call button to the hours calls connect, replace weak ad lines.

Day 30

Review leads by town and search with you, move money toward real jobs, and check that your free deck leads near home held up.

Months 2 and 3

Tell Google which leads became jobs, then decide on more budget. If you have an insurance certificate, we can also apply for Google's Local Services Ads, which charge per lead.

When we'd change course

  • Your deck leads near home drop, or a competitor starts running ads above your map listing: we turn deck ads back on there at a low bid.
  • Leads from far-off towns don't turn into jobs: we lower bids there and move the money closer.
  • Calls go unanswered: we sort that out with you before adding budget, because more budget would only buy more missed calls.

The numbers here are estimates, not a guarantee. Google sets click prices in a live auction, and results change with the season and with how quickly calls get answered.
