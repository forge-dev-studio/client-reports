What this measures

When someone searches for a car near them, Google shows a short list of local dealerships above the regular results. That short list is where the phone calls come from. Being fourth means being invisible.

Each search combined a term with a town, for example used cars rome ga or car dealership cartersville ga. We recorded Cochran's position in the map list, the position of the website in the regular results, and what Google's AI answer said.

These are Google Search results, not Google Maps. Rankings inside Maps lean heavily on how close the searcher happens to be standing, and Cochran sits on North Broad with a good address, so Maps flatters the picture. Search is the harder, fairer measure and the one a buyer two towns over actually sees.

Two numbers below were checked by hand in a browser as well as by the measurement tool, because they disagreed. The short list of local dealerships Google prints on the results page was confirmed identical both ways, down to the ratings and review counts. Cochran's exact position in the longer list behind that short list could not be measured reliably from outside Google, so it is deliberately not reported here rather than reported wrongly. That figure is being taken from Google's own profile data instead.

This is a baseline. It is the measurement everything after it gets compared against.

Google now answers car-shopping questions with an AI summary written from a handful of websites it chooses to read. We ran all 180 searches through it and recorded every source each answer used.

Google returned an AI answer on all 180 searches. Cochran Motor Company was named in 33 of them, and the Cochran website was cited as a source in 7.

Where this leaves us

This baseline exists so the next measurement means something. Three things it establishes:

The short list is the gap. Google printed its three-dealership local list on 161 of 180 searches, and that block is where a car buyer's attention lands first. Cochran is not in it on any of them. He does appear in the longer list behind it, which is why he turns up when you go looking; that position is being read from Google's own profile data rather than guessed at from outside.

The website reaches Rome and stops. All 11 of its appearances in the regular results are Rome searches. Across the other eight towns inside 30 miles it does not reach the first twenty listings on any of the 20 searches.

AI answers are now the whole surface, not part of it. Google produced one on every search we ran. Cochran is named in 33 of them but its website is read in only 7, so most of what the AI says about the dealership is currently coming from somewhere other than the dealership.

The next measurement will use the same 20 searches across the same 9 towns, so the comparison is like for like.
